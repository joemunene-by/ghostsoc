# Security policy

ghostsoc runs other security tools and handles their findings, which may include
secrets and sensitive host data. Treat its reports as sensitive.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email joemunene984@gmail.com with details and a way to reproduce. Please do not
open a public issue for a vulnerability. I aim to acknowledge within a few days.

## Scope and intent

ghostsoc is for authorised assessment of systems you own or have permission to
test. The adapters only invoke tools already on your PATH; ghostsoc does not
exploit anything itself. You are responsible for having authorisation to scan a
given target.
